## 🎯 Key Decision Points

When implementing, you'll need to decide:

1. **Email Templates**: Customize Mailtrap templates (guide in `docs/MAILTRAP_SETUP_GUIDE.md`)
2. **UI Colors**: Customize modern palette (in `lib/views/widgets/modern_colors.dart`)
3. **Notification Types**: Choose which to enable (in notification preferences)
4. **Update Frequency**: Real-time or batched (in controllers)
5. **Retention**: How long to keep notifications (add to settings)
